PRIVACY

Local by design.

Cut IQ Studio does not include analytics, telemetry, advertising, or an account system. Project metadata is stored in a MariaDB instance bound to your own computer. Finished media is written to a folder you control.

The app server and bundled database bind to 127.0.0.1. Google Drive and rclone integrations are disabled unless you configure and choose them.

When you use YouTube or another source service, that service receives the network requests required to retrieve metadata, captions, or media. Those requests are governed by the source service's terms and privacy policy.

Uninstalling Cut IQ does not silently delete your project database or exported clips.
